Agent Monitor

amon runs your agent untouched — no multiplexer or dashboard. You know when it is working, idle, or needs your attention. Built for Omarchy Linux.

omarchy + amon = your agent orchestrator

You don't need another environment to manage your agents — Omarchy already gave you the perfect container. A workspace holds everything you need: agent terminals, a browser, the logs — tiled or side-scrolling, however you like to work.

amon just adds the last missing piece: showing you which agent needs your attention, at a glance, beautifully integrated.
